HeadVoice turns an ordinary webcam into a voice. Tilt your head to choose a message, hold a long blink to say it, and your computer speaks for you. Nothing is said without your own blink.

Nothing is said without you

A message is spoken only after your own long blink on it. Two quick blinks cancel. If the camera loses you, nothing happens.

Fits how you move

A short calibration measures how far you can tilt and how you blink, then picks the layout and settings that suit you. It keeps adjusting as you tire.

Video stays on your computer

The camera picture is processed on your own computer and never stored. Only simple numbers, like how far you tilted, are used to tune the app.

How it works

  1. 1

    Look at the screen

    Sit comfortably in front of any webcam. HeadVoice learns your resting position in a second.

  2. 2

    Tilt to choose

    Tip your head toward a message to highlight it. Small movements are fine.

  3. 3

    Blink to say it

    Close both eyes for a moment. The ring fills, and your computer says the message out loud.
