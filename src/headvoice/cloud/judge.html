Adaptation decisions

Research and education prototype, not a medical device. Replay uses recorded measurements, so it cannot show how a person would react to a changed layout. Fatigue is simulated by shrinking recorded head movements over the session. Sessions marked "Simulated user" come from the simulated-users study (a model of a user driving the real app), not from recordings of people.
